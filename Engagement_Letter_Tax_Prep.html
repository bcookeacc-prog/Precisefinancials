Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide complete, accurate, and timely tax documents, including but not limited to:

  • W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and all income statements
  • Receipts and records for deductions claimed
  • Prior year tax return (if applicable)
  • Social Security numbers for all individuals on the return
  • Bank account and routing numbers for direct deposit of any refund
  • Any IRS notices, correspondence, or prior audit information

The Client is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of all information provided. Precise Financials, LLC will prepare returns solely based on Client-provided information without independent verification. The Client must review and approve the completed return before it is submitted for filing.

Important: By signing below, the Client confirms that all information provided is accurate and complete to the best of their knowledge. Filing a return with knowingly false information is a federal offense.

IRS e-File & Signatures

Precise Financials, LLC is an IRS Authorized e-File Provider. All eligible returns will be filed electronically. The Client will be required to sign IRS Form 8879 (e-File Signature Authorization) before the return is transmitted to the IRS. The Firm will not file any return without the Client's signed authorization.

Firm Responsibilities

  • Prepare returns accurately based on information provided by the Client.
  • Notify the Client of any missing documents or information needed to complete the return.
  • Provide the Client with a copy of the completed return for review before filing.
  • File the return electronically upon receiving signed Form 8879 and full payment.
  • Maintain confidentiality of all Client tax and financial information.
  • Retain Client records for a minimum of 7 years.

Confidentiality

All tax and financial information shared by the Client is held in strict confidence and will not be disclosed to any third party without written consent, except as required by law or a valid IRS or court order. Precise Financials, LLC uses industry-standard data security practices to protect all Client data.

The Firm is not responsible for errors resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or untimely information provided by the Client. The Firm is not liable for any taxes, penalties, or interest assessed by the IRS or any taxing authority resulting from inaccurate Client-provided information. Total liability shall not exceed the fees paid for the specific service giving rise to the claim.
