Client Responsibilities

To allow the Firm to perform services effectively, the Client agrees to:

  • Provide accurate, complete, and timely financial records, documents, and information as requested.
  • Notify the Firm promptly of any changes in business operations, ownership, or financial circumstances that may affect the services.
  • Review all work product delivered and communicate any discrepancies or corrections within 10 business days.
  • Maintain proper records and retain copies of all documents provided to the Firm.
  • Respond to requests for information in a timely manner to avoid delays in deliverables.

Firm Responsibilities

Precise Financials, LLC agrees to:

  • Perform the agreed-upon services with reasonable care, competence, and professionalism.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality of all Client financial information in accordance with applicable professional standards.
  • Communicate proactively regarding the status of work, deadlines, and any issues that arise.
  • Retain Client records for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with standard record-keeping practices.

All information shared by the Client with Precise Financials, LLC is held in strict confidence and will not be disclosed to any third party without written consent from the Client, except as required by law or regulatory authority.
